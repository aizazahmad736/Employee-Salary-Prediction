💡 What's happening behind the scenes?

1. Categorical Variable Encoding

Our Linear Regression model can't process words. So we encoded them:

• Ordinal Encoding for Education: Map categories to ordered integers (0 to 3) because PhD > Master's > Bachelor's > High School.
• One-Hot Encoding for Job Role and Location: Binary indicator columns (0 or 1) are created. We drop the first category (e.g. Director & Metro) to act as the baseline reference in the intercept.

2. Linear Regression Formula

The model predicts salary by adding or subtracting weights based on your selection:

Salary = Base Intercept + (Experience * 3190.58) + (Education_Encoded * 13870.17) + Job_Role_Coefficient + Location_Coefficient
